# Worked Example — ICP for "LedgerLoop"

> A fully fictional example to show what a finished SuperICP output looks like.
> **LedgerLoop** is an (imaginary) AI bookkeeping tool for Shopify brands.
> Mode: `reverse-engineered` · Last reviewed: `2026-06-28`

---

## 🎯 The one-liner (comically narrow)

> Shopify brands doing **$1M–$8M/year** that just crossed **10k orders/month**,
> still close their books in **spreadsheets**, and have **no in-house accountant**.
> `[EVIDENCE]` — matches 7 of our 8 best-retained accounts.

## 👤 The named avatar

- **Tag:** "Overwhelmed Olivia"
- **Role & context:** Founder/CEO of a 9-person DTC skincare brand. Wears the
  finance hat she never wanted. Books are 6 weeks behind.
- **The moment she needs us:** Her accountant emails at tax time asking for a P&L
  by SKU and she realizes she has no idea what her margins actually are.

## 🧠 The job they're hiring us for (JTBD)

- **Functional:** Close the books accurately without touching a spreadsheet.
- **Emotional:** Stop feeling anxious and "behind" about money.
- **Social:** Look like a real CEO who knows their numbers in front of investors.
- **Struggling moment:** Month-end, staring at a Shopify export at 11pm, unsure if
  they made money. `[EVIDENCE]`
- **Four forces:** Push = books always late & wrong · Pull = "real-time margins" ·
  Anxiety = "will it sync my 2 years of history correctly?" · Habit = the
  spreadsheet they've limped along with for years.

## 🚫 Anti-ICP — refuse these

| Disqualifier | Why it fails | Hard rule |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-$500k revenue | Too small, churns on price | Disqualify < $500k |
| Already on NetSuite / has a controller | Solved problem, we lose to incumbent | Disqualify |
| Services/agency (no inventory) | Our COGS engine adds no value | Route away |
| B2B wholesale-only | Different accounting model | Disqualify |
