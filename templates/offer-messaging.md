# Offer & Messaging Module (optional)

Run this *after* the ICP is built. It consumes the ICP's **pains**, **dream
outcome (gains)**, **objections**, and **before → after** grid and turns them into
an offer people feel stupid saying no to, plus messaging that lands.

> Principle: the ICP tells you *who* and *why they buy*. This module turns that
> into *what you sell* and *what you say*.

---

## Part A — The Offer (Hormozi Value Equation)

> **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

Raise the top, shrink the bottom.

| Lever | Question | From the ICP |
|---|---|---|
| ⬆️ Dream outcome | What's the "After" they crave? Sell the destination, not the vehicle. | Gains / After grid |
| ⬆️ Perceived likelihood | Why will they believe it'll work *for them*? (proof, testimonials, guarantee) | Anxiety force / objections |
| ⬇️ Time delay | How fast is the first win? Can you engineer a week-one result? | Push / trigger |
| ⬇️ Effort & sacrifice | How do we make it done-for-you / dead-simple? | Pains |

**Build the Grand Slam Offer:**

1. **List every problem** standing between the customer and their dream outcome
   (pull straight from the ICP pains).
2. **Convert each problem into a deliverable** that solves it.
3. **Stack** the deliverables and name + price each so the value is visible.
4. **Guarantee** — remove the risk (the bigger the better, and honest).
5. **Scarcity & urgency** — real, not fake (limited slots, cohort, deadline).

## Part C — Match the message to awareness level

Tune *how much you explain* to where the customer's head is at (Eugene Schwartz):

| Level | They know… | Lead with… |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | nothing | the problem / a story |
| Problem-aware | they have the pain | empathy + naming the pain |
| Solution-aware | solutions exist | why your approach wins |
| Product-aware | your product exists | proof, differentiation, offer |
| Most-aware | they want it | the deal, urgency, CTA |
